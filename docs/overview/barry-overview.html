Features 1 of 2

Before You Go and While You Are Out

Tell Barry where you are going and when you will be back. If you don't check in by then, Barry tells the people you chose.

Before You Go

A Plan and a Deadline

Destination, route and the time you will be back.

Emergency Contacts

Chosen per adventure from a saved list. They need no app and no account. Barry reaches them by email, text message, or both.

A Heads-Up at the Start

Each contact learns that you are heading out, with your plan, your deadline and their own link to your adventure page.

Plans for Later

Save an adventure for later and start it with one tap when you leave.

While You Are Out

Your Trail

With location access set to Always, Barry records a position about once a minute and keeps readings on the phone without signal.

A Live Adventure Page

Contacts see your trail, last known position and plan at barry.support/adventure. It updates every minute and never shows the deadline.

Need More Time?

Move the deadline later with one tap, even when you are already overdue. Edit the plan, and contacts get a plan update.

Reminder and Overdue Notice

The phone itself reminds you 30 minutes before the deadline and tells you when you are overdue, even without signal.

Features 2 of 2

Coming Back, or Not in Time

Barry's server keeps the time, so it raises the alarm even when your phone is off or out of signal.

Coming back

Hold to Check In

A deliberate press and hold, so a pocket cannot check you in by accident. A check-in without signal is kept and sent when the phone is online again.

Coming back

Contacts See You Are Back

After the check-in the adventure page shows the time you checked in, with your trail up to then.

Not in time

An Alert to Every Contact

One minute after the deadline, then every hour, up to three alerts. Only an alert carries your phone number, car and medical notes.

Coming back

An All-Clear

When you check in, on time or late, every contact hears that you are back safe.

Not in time

Messages That Did Not Arrive

If an email or text message fails, the app shows you which contact did not get it. Contacts who share an address get one message.

Your data

History and Deleting

Past adventures with plan, check-in time and trail. Delete an adventure or the whole account, and the contacts' links stop working.

Barry is not an emergency service. It never calls rescue services. It tells the people you chose, and they decide what to do.

The Components of Barry

iPhone

The Barry App

  • Expo SDK 58, React Native, Expo Router
  • Tabs: Adventure, History, Contacts, Profile
  • Liquid Glass buttons through SwiftUI
  • Background trail with a 200 m geofence
  • Local Reminder and Overdue Notice
  • Offline check-in queue
Supabase

Backend and Clock

  • Postgres schema with row-level security
  • 37 migrations, commands as functions
  • A clock in pg_cron, every minute
  • Auth with email confirmation
  • Vault for the delivery secret
Edge Function

deliver

  • Renders heads-up, plan update, deadline update, alert and all-clear
  • Email through Resend as "🐶 Barry"
  • Text messages through Twilio, with delivery reports
  • Retries and delivery failures
barry.support

Web Pages

  • Landing page with all features
  • Adventure page for contacts, with an OpenStreetMap trail
  • Email confirmation page
  • Published by a GitHub Pages workflow
Quality

Tests

  • 15 database test files (pgTAP)
  • Unit tests for the app (Jest)
  • Tests for deliver (Deno)
  • 11 end-to-end scripts against the local stack
Tooling

Scripts and Skills

Steps to a Production-Ready App

Join the Apple Developer Program

A paid team for TestFlight and the App Store. Today the app is installed by hand.

Set up release builds

eas.json has the build profiles. Add version numbers and TestFlight testers once the paid team exists.

Prepare the App Store listing

Screenshots, privacy labels and the reason for "Always" location for App Review.

Move Twilio to production

A trial account reaches only verified numbers. Upgrade it and use the alphanumeric sender "Barry".

Upgrade the Supabase plan

Daily backups, no pausing, and a higher limit for sign-up emails.

Monitor the clock and delivery

Set HEALTHCHECK_URL to an uptime monitor and report app errors.

Rotate exposed keys

Rotate the Resend key and delete the Azure Communication Services resource.

Run every test in CI

Database, Deno, unit and end-to-end tests on each push, not only the Pages deploy.

Merge or close open branches

The Plan screen fix, the Maestro end-to-end tests and the check-in wake branch.
