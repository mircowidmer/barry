### The stack cannot give these

**E10. An Alert goes out before every other message that is waiting to be sent.**
- `docs/spec.md:151` "An Alert goes out before every other message that is waiting to be sent, so a queue of other messages cannot hold it back."
- Below: pgTAP `limits.test.sql:70-80` ("an Alert is claimed before an older message of another kind").
- Sketch, `e2e/alert-goes-out-first.e2e.mjs`: queue a backlog of older Heads-ups with `admin`, miss the Deadline, run `deliver` once with a claim limit of one, and assert the Alert left first.
- Outcome: **skipped**, rule 2. `runDeliver` drains the whole outbox in one run, and the order Mailpit receives a batch in is not the order of the claim, so the case would pass or fail by timing (rule 5). **E11. Supabase refuses a password change from a sign-in older than 24 hours.**

- Outcome: **skipped**, rule 2. Aging a session means rewriting `auth.sessions` and `auth.users` rows that Auth, not the clock, writes.
