After `node scripts/maplibre-worker.mjs` the page loaded with no console error.

It was created through the app's own **Create Account** with the values of `.env.local`, which is `signin-create`, and Luca (`luca@example.com`) and Sara (`+41792222222`) were inserted as the baseline precondition the feature index names.
