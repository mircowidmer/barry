## 2. Threat model

- **Assets:** the safety promise (an Alert goes out when a Deadline passes and only the Adventurer can stop it); the live Trail, the destination and the Deadline; the Profile with its medical notes and the contacts' addresses; the sessions; the budget and reputation of Resend and Twilio; the operator's machines and secrets.
- **Boundaries:** the phone and PostgREST through the publishable key and the user's JWT, with only `public` and `graphql_public` exposed; the Postgres roles `anon`, `authenticated`, `service_role` and the definers of `commands` and `private`; `deliver` with `verify_jwt = false`, gated by `DELIVER_SECRET` and the HMAC report token; Twilio, Resend and Auth's SMTP; Open-Meteo and the SLF for the Conditions, Apple Maps for the place search, Cloudflare Turnstile; barry.support on GitHub Pages with OpenFreeMap tiles; CI on GitHub-hosted runners and, for `main` and this repository's pull requests, the self-hosted Mac `barry-mac`.
- **Attackers:** another Adventurer with their own session reaches PostgREST and every command; an anonymous caller reaches `trail_map`, `deliver` and Auth; an Emergency Contact holds a token; someone near the phone reaches its storage and backups; anyone on GitHub can fork the public repository; the supply chain reaches npm, Deno and the Actions.

## 3. Findings

### SEC-2 · The cached Adventure, the Trail buffer and the Check-in queue sit in a storage file that iOS backups include

- **Severity:** Low. CWE-312, Cleartext Storage of Sensitive Information. OWASP MASVS-STORAGE-1.
- **Where:** `src/lib/storage.ts:1-3` (`import 'expo-sqlite/localStorage/install'; export const storage = localStorage;`), read by `src/lib/barry/adventure-cache.ts`, `trail-buffer.ts`, `check-in-queue.ts` and `conditions.ts`.
- **Attack path:** someone with an unencrypted backup of the phone, or with a jailbroken phone, opens the app container's SQLite file and reads the last Adventure with its destination and Deadline, the Trail readings waiting for signal, and the rounded points of the last five destinations.
- **What they gain:** where the Adventurer went and planned to go. The session itself is in the Keychain since 23 October, so no access to the account.
- **Certainty:** certain that the file holds them; the file protection class could not be checked without a device.
- **Fix:** the author's decision between `expo-secure-store` for the small values (the cache, the queue) and accepting the file for the Trail buffer, which can hold hundreds of readings; or `expo-file-system` with `NSFileProtectionComplete` for all four.
- **Outcome:** **closed by decision** on 30 October 2026. The author accepted that a backup of the phone includes these stores, on the grounds that the Adventurer's own phone is where the data belongs and the session is already in the Keychain. `docs/spec.md` § Accounts states what the backup includes and what someone who opens it learns. No code changed; the finding does not carry over.
