Barry is not an emergency service. It never calls rescue services, and it cannot guarantee that a message arrives. In an emergency, call the rescue services yourself: 112, or 1414 for Rega in Switzerland.

The Service

Barry is run by Mirco Widmer as a private person (help@barry.support). Barry tells the emergency contacts you choose when you don't check in from an adventure by the time you set. It is free and still in development. Features can change, and Barry can be paused or ended. If Barry ends, you will be told beforehand where possible.

What Barry Cannot Promise

Barry depends on things outside its control: your phone's battery, signal and settings, the internet, the providers that deliver emails and text messages, and your contacts reading them. A message can arrive late or not at all, and your last position can be old or inaccurate. Barry is an additional safety net, never a replacement for careful planning, telling people your plans yourself, and your own judgement.

Your Part

  • You must be at least 16 years old.
  • Give accurate details, keep your password to yourself, and keep your plan and the time you will be back up to date.
  • Add only people as emergency contacts who agreed to it, and tell them what Barry will send them.
  • Don't use Barry to send messages to people who did not agree, or to disturb or deceive anyone. Accounts that do can be blocked and deleted.

Liability

To the extent permitted by law, any liability for damage resulting from using Barry, or from Barry not working, is excluded. This includes messages that arrive late or not at all. Liability for intent and gross negligence remains.

Your Data

How Barry handles your data and your contacts' data is described in the Privacy Policy. You can delete your account at any time in the app.

Changes and Law

If these terms change, the new version is published here with its date, and you will be told in the app if the change matters to you. Swiss law applies. The place of jurisdiction is Uster, Switzerland, unless the law gives you another.
