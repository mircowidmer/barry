Barry

Go alone, get home.

Your watchdog for adventures alone. Tell Barry where you are going and when you will be back. If you don't check in by then, Barry tells the people you chose.

For iPhone. In development, not yet in the App Store.

Before You Go

Plan the adventure in a minute.

  • A Plan and a DeadlineWhere you are going, your route, where you start, notes such as what you are wearing, and the time you will be back.
  • Emergency ContactsChoose people from your saved list for each adventure. They need no app and no account. Barry reaches them by email, by text message, or both.
  • A Heads-Up When You StartYour contacts learn that you are heading out, with your plan, your deadline and their own link to your trail map.
  • Plans for LaterSave an adventure and start it when you leave.

While You Are Out

Barry keeps an eye on you without you having to think about it.

  • Your TrailWith location access set to Always, Barry records where you are about once a minute. Without signal it keeps the readings on your phone and sends them later.
  • A Live Trail Map for Your ContactsA web page with your trail, your last known position and your plan. It updates every minute and never shows your deadline.
  • Need More Time?Move your deadline later with one tap, even when you are already overdue.
  • Plans ChangeEdit your plan on the way, and your contacts get a plan update. Add or remove contacts: a new contact gets a heads-up, and a removed contact's link stops working. All of this works until the first alert.
  • A Reminder and an Overdue NoticeYour phone reminds you 30 minutes before the deadline and tells you when you are overdue, even without signal.

Coming Back

One deliberate gesture and you are done.

  • Hold to Check InA short press is not enough, so a pocket cannot check you in by accident.
  • Check In Without SignalBarry keeps your check-in and sends it as soon as your phone has signal again.
  • Your Contacts See You Are BackAfter the check-in their trail map shows the time you checked in and no positions.

If You Don't Come Back in Time

Barry's server keeps the time, so it raises the alarm even when your phone is off or out of signal.

  • An Alert to Every ContactOne minute after your deadline, then every hour, up to three alerts. Each one carries your plan, your last known position with a map link, your phone number, your car and your medical notes. No other message carries your phone number, car or medical notes.
  • An All-ClearWhen you check in after an alert, every contact who got one hears that you are back safe.
  • Messages That Did Not ArriveIf an email or text message to a contact fails, the app shows you which one.

Your Data

Your positions belong to you.

  • HistoryLook back at past adventures with their plan, check-in time and trail.
  • Delete What You WantDelete a past or planned adventure, or your whole account with everything in it. The trail map links stop working.
  • Only the People You ChoseYour contacts see your trail only during the adventure, through their own link. Nobody else does.

Barry is not an emergency service. It never calls rescue services. It tells the people you chose, and they decide what to do.

Barry is named after the famous St. Bernard rescue dog of the Great St. Bernard Pass.
